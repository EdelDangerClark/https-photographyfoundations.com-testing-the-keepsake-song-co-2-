Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to send you?
+
Once you've gone through checkout, you'll receive a link with everything I need from you. Nothing overwhelming, just the key details that matter, like who or what the song is for and the moments you'd like included. You can send a voice note about the person too.
How long does it take?
+
Turnaround is 3–7 days from when I receive your details.
Can I hear it before it's finished?
+
Yes. You'll get to preview your song first, so you know you love it before it's finalised.
What if I don't love it?
+
If anything needs changing after your preview, one revision is included. If you're not happy with your preview, you get a full refund. Once your finished song has been delivered, all digital sales are final and no refunds are given. Your happiness really matters to me, though, so if something still isn't right after your revision, get in touch and I'll do everything I can to put it right.
What will I receive?
+
A completely custom song, a written lyric version and a full MP3.
What occasions is it for?
+
Birthdays, partners, children, anniversaries, special memories and even emotional tribute pieces. If there's a story behind it, it can become a song.
Can I choose the style of the song?
+
Yes. You can choose the genre, a male or female singer, the type of mood, and more.
Can I get my song faster?
+
Yes. Rush delivery within 48 hours is a £9.99 add-on. Buy it from the add-ons under the prices straight after your order.
Can I order a song for Christmas?
+
Yes. Christmas orders need to be in by 16 December, so your song is ready in time.
Can you make a song in memory of someone?
+
Yes. Some of the most meaningful songs are tribute pieces. Share the memories that matter most and I'll create something gentle and personal to honour them.
How are the songs made?
+
I use AI to help capture all the details of your person and produce the music. Then the writer in me takes over. I'm a qualified journalist and English teacher, and I've written poems and songs all my life. I revise, refine and rewrite the lyrics until they sound exactly as they should. That way you get truly personal words with heart and meaning, not generic “AI slop”, at a fraction of a studio price.
Is it really written just for us?
+
Yes. Every song is written from scratch around your story. No templates, no name swapped into an existing track.
Can I get more than one version?
+
Yes. The Keepsake Bundle includes a second version in a different style, so you can pick a favourite. With the song on its own, you can buy an extra style version for £9.99 from the add-ons under the prices.
What does it cost?
+
The Keepsake Song is £34.99. The Keepsake Bundle is £44.99 and adds a lyric artwork with your own photo and a second style version. Right now, the first 15 orders get the full bundle for £34.99. When I had my daughter's song made, it cost over £250. I wanted every family to be able to have one.
