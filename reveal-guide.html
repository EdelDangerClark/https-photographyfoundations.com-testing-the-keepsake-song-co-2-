The Christmas Reveal Guide

You've got the song. Now for the moment. Here are five ways to play it so it lands the way you hope, whether it's for Mum, your partner, or in memory of someone you love.

I put my heart into every line of your song. Now it's your turn to give it. These are my favourite ways to make the moment special.Edel, The Keepsake Song Co.

1

After the presents

Wait until the wrapping paper is everywhere and everyone has slowed down. Then say "There's one more present, and you can't unwrap it." Press play. The quiet after the rush makes the words land.

2

At the dinner table

Play it between the main course and pudding, when everyone is together and sitting still. Turn the music off first, so the song is the only sound in the room.

3

On a video call for family far away

Share your screen and your sound, then play it while you watch their face. Tip: send the MP3 afterwards so they can play it again on their own.

4

Hidden in a card

Write "Press play before you read the rest" inside a card, with a QR code or the link to the song. Lovely for someone who'd rather have their moment privately.

5

On the car journey home

For a quieter moment, play it on the drive back from a family visit. No audience, no pressure, just the two of you and their story.

A few small tips

  • Test the volume first. Phone speakers are fine, but a small speaker makes it feel like an event.
  • Have tissues nearby. Truly.
  • Film their reaction if they'd be happy with that. Lots of people love watching it back.
  • For a memorial song, choose a quiet moment and let the person listen in their own way. There's no right way to hear it.
