6. How your song is made

We're open about our process. AI tools help us gather the details of your person, and they produce the music and vocals. The lyrics are written, revised and refined by Edel until they're right for the person they're for. The singing voice is AI-generated, not a recording of a real singer.

Every song is created individually for you. Because songs are made in popular styles, your song may share general musical features (a genre, a feel, a common chord pattern) with other songs. It won't copy any existing song.

Please send your feedback within 14 days of receiving your preview. A revision can't change the whole song into a different song; a completely new brief would be a new order. Extra changes after your included revision may be charged, and we'll always agree any cost with you first.

Refund Policy

We want you to love your song. Here's exactly what happens if you change your mind or aren't happy.

Before work starts

If you cancel before you've sent us your story, you'll get a full refund.

At the preview stage

You'll hear your song before it's final, with one revision included. If you don't love your preview, tell us before we deliver your final song, and within 14 days of receiving your preview, and we'll give you a full refund of your song or bundle. No awkward questions.

After final delivery

Once your final song has been delivered, we can't offer a refund, because it's a personalised digital product made only for you. If anything is faulty or not as described, we'll put it right, as explained in section 11 above.

Add-ons

If you cancel before work starts, add-ons are refunded in full. If we miss a rush delivery deadline for a reason within our control, we'll refund the rush fee. An extra style version that's been delivered can't be refunded.

How to ask for a refund

Email TheKeepsakeSongCo@creativemamamedia.com with your name and order details. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 14 days of us agreeing them.
